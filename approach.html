Our Approach

A transparent process, from first inquiry to finished work.

Every project follows the same five stages, though the specific research and recommendations are different for every book. Here's exactly what happens at each step, and what you can expect along the way.

The Guiding Framework

Catalogue Insight → Reader Opportunity → Strategic Curiosity

This underlying order shapes everything in the five stages below. Research always comes before recommendation.

Step One

Catalogue Insight

Understand the book, the catalogue, and the genre context before forming any opinion.

Step Two

Reader Opportunity

Identify where relevant readers are likely to be and what realistic pathways exist.

Step Three

Strategic Curiosity

Build a plan around what's genuinely worth trying next for this specific book.

01

Understand the Author and the Work

Every project begins with a careful review of the book itself. This means reading the book's description, examining its genre and subgenre positioning, reviewing any available sample pages or summaries, and understanding the intended audience as the author sees it. I also look at the author's broader catalogue, if one exists, and any publicly visible information about how the book has been presented so far.

This stage is about observation, not evaluation. The goal is to build an accurate picture of what the book is and who it's for, before forming any opinions about what should happen next.

What I Review

Book description, genre, sample content, author catalogue, and existing public positioning.

What I Ask You

About your goals, your intended readers, and anything you've already tried.

What You Receive

A summary of how I understand your book's current positioning and audience.

02

Identify Discovery Opportunities

With a clear understanding of the book in place, I turn to identifying where opportunity actually exists. This involves examining relevant positioning on retail and reader platforms, reviewing how your catalogue connects across titles, and researching reader communities, categories, and promotional channels that are genuinely relevant to your genre and audience.

Not every opportunity I find will be worth pursuing, and I'll be direct about which ones seem most promising based on what the research actually shows, rather than listing every possible tactic regardless of fit.

Platform Review

Amazon and Goodreads positioning, categories, and visible discovery signals.

Catalogue Mapping

How your books relate to one another and where cross-discovery could improve.

Reader Pathways

Relevant communities, comparable titles, and realistic promotional channels.

03

Develop a Tailored Strategy

This is where the research comes together into a practical plan. Rather than applying the same set of marketing activities to every author, I recommend specific actions based on what's been found during the first two stages. The strategy reflects your book's genre, your available time and budget, and the realistic opportunities identified through research.

You'll receive a written strategy document outlining recommended priorities, a suggested sequence of activities, and the reasoning behind each recommendation, so you understand not just what to do, but why it's being suggested.

Prioritized Actions

A ranked list of recommendations based on research findings, not a generic checklist.

Clear Reasoning

An explanation of why each recommendation applies specifically to your book.

Realistic Scope

A plan shaped by your actual time, budget, and publishing situation.

04

Agree on Scope and Pricing

Before any work begins, we discuss which parts of the recommended strategy you'd like to move forward with, what the deliverables will look like, what timeline is realistic, and what the fees will be. Nothing proceeds without a clear, mutual understanding of scope, so there are no surprises about cost or deliverables once work is underway.

This stage is also an opportunity to ask questions, adjust priorities, or scale the project up or down based on your budget and goals.

Defined Deliverables

A clear written description of exactly what will be delivered.

Agreed Timeline

Realistic milestones based on the scope of work and your availability.

Transparent Fees

Pricing discussed and confirmed before any work begins.

05

Execute and Review

Once scope is agreed, I carry out the work: whether that's a strategy document, a visibility review, campaign coordination, creative materials, or outreach activity. Throughout the project, I review available feedback and performance information where applicable, and share updates on progress at agreed intervals.

Research informs every recommendation I make, but it doesn't guarantee a particular commercial outcome. Reader behaviour, platform algorithms, and market conditions are outside anyone's direct control. What I can offer is thoughtful, well-researched work and honest communication about what the available information suggests.

Agreed Delivery

Work carried out according to the scope and timeline we've confirmed together.

Progress Updates

Regular communication so you know where the project stands.

Honest Review

A look at available feedback or performance data, without overstating what it means.

An Honest Note

Research improves decisions. It doesn't guarantee results.

I won't promise bestseller status, a specific number of sales, guaranteed reviews, or award recognition, because no one can honestly make those promises. What I can commit to is thorough research, clear reasoning, and strategies built around your book's actual positioning rather than generic templates.
