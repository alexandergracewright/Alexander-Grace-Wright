The Challenge Most Authors Face

Before strategy comes clarity. These are the patterns that show up again and again, regardless of genre or publishing path.

Positioning Is Unclear

Many authors have not clearly mapped where their book fits among comparable titles, which makes every marketing decision harder than it needs to be.

Catalogue Connections Go Unused

Backlist titles, related works, and natural reader pathways often sit unexamined, leaving existing opportunities on the table.

Discovery Is Scattered

Readers who would genuinely enjoy a book often never encounter it, not because the book lacks merit, but because discovery efforts are unfocused.
