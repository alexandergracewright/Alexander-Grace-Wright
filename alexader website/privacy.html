Privacy Policy

A plain-language explanation of what information is collected through this site and how it is used.

Last updated: this policy applies to the inquiry form and general browsing of this website. It does not apply to any third-party sites linked from this website.

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected and used when you visit this website or submit the inquiry form on the Discuss Your Book page. This site is operated by Alexander Grace Wright, a book marketing strategy and reader discovery consulting practice.

This policy is written in plain language to help visitors understand what happens with the information they choose to share. It is intended as a general, good-faith description of current practices rather than a formal legal document, and it does not assert compliance with any specific data protection law or regulatory framework.

Information Collected

This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics software to monitor visitor behavior. The only information actively collected is what you choose to submit through the inquiry form on the Discuss Your Book page.

Information You Submit Through the Form

  • Your name and email address
  • Your author website or book link, if provided
  • Information about your publishing background and the service you are interested in
  • A description of your book, marketing goals, or challenges, if provided
  • Optional information such as budget range or additional notes you choose to include

You are never asked to provide sensitive personal information, government identification numbers, or payment or financial details through this form, and you should not submit such information.

How Information Is Used

Information submitted through the inquiry form is used solely to respond to your inquiry, evaluate whether there is a good fit for working together, and follow up about the services described on this website. Information you provide is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Occasionally, anonymized and general observations about common author challenges may inform the content of this website or future communications. No identifying details, book titles, or personal information are used in this way without your separate permission.

Role of the Form Processing Provider

The inquiry form on this website is processed using Formspree, a third-party form handling service. When you submit the form, your information is transmitted to Formspree's servers and then forwarded by email to Alexander Grace Wright.

Formspree operates independently and maintains its own privacy practices, security measures, and data retention policies, which are outside the direct control of this website. If you would like to understand how Formspree handles data on its platform, please refer to Formspree's own privacy policy and terms of service directly on their website.

This site does not independently verify or guarantee Formspree's internal data handling practices. Using the form means your submitted information will pass through Formspree's infrastructure before reaching this business.

Data Handling and Retention

Reasonable care is taken to treat information submitted through the inquiry form as confidential and to use it only for the purposes described above. Inquiry information is generally retained only as long as needed to respond to your request, evaluate a potential working relationship, or satisfy basic recordkeeping for ongoing client work.

No website can guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet. While reasonable precautions are taken, this policy does not claim a verified or certified security standard, and no representation is made that information will be protected against every possible risk.

Confidentiality of Author and Book Information

Details you share about your book, your publishing history, or your marketing goals are treated as confidential business information. As described on the Selected Work page of this website, client names, book titles, sales figures, and private communications are not shared publicly or with other parties without explicit permission.

Your Choices

  • You may choose not to submit the inquiry form, or to omit any optional fields within it.
  • You may request that information you previously submitted be deleted by contacting the email address below.
  • You may ask questions at any time about what information has been received and how it has been used.

Children's Privacy

This website and its services are intended for authors, publishers, and publishing professionals. It is not directed toward children, and the inquiry form is not knowingly used to collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in how this website or the inquiry form operates. Any changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of the inquiry form after changes are posted indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions About This Policy

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or about how information you submit is handled, please reach out directly.
