Discuss Your Book
Services

Six areas of focused work, scoped to your book and your goals.

Every service begins with research specific to your catalogue and genre. None of these are offered as standalone packages applied the same way to every author. Scope and sequencing are discussed during the inquiry process.

You don't need to know which service is right for your book before reaching out. Most projects begin with a conversation about your goals, followed by research that informs which of these areas, or which combination, makes sense for your specific situation.

01
Strategy

Book Discovery & Marketing Strategy

Many authors are unsure where to start because generic marketing advice doesn't account for their book's genre, audience, or current positioning. This service addresses that gap directly. I review your book's description, comparable titles, intended readership, and existing visibility, then build a strategy that reflects what I actually find, not a template applied regardless of the book.

What you can expect: a clear picture of your book's current positioning, a set of realistic opportunities worth pursuing, and a prioritized plan that accounts for your timeline and resources.

Discuss Your Strategy

This Service Addresses

  • Unclear sense of who the intended reader actually is
  • Uncertainty about which marketing activities are worth the time
  • A need for a plan grounded in the book itself, not trends
02
Research

Author & Catalogue Research

Authors with more than one book often have untapped connections between titles that readers never discover on their own. This service involves a close review of your full catalogue, including series order, recurring themes, tone, and subject matter, to identify pathways through which a reader of one book might naturally find another.

What you can expect: a mapped-out view of how your titles relate to one another, and recommendations for how that relationship could be made clearer to readers across your author platform, retail pages, and promotional materials.

Discuss Your Catalogue

This Service Addresses

  • Readers who finish one book but don't discover the next
  • Series or backlist titles with low cross-visibility
  • Unclear thematic throughlines across an author's body of work
03
Visibility Review

Amazon & Goodreads Visibility

Retail and reader platforms have their own discovery mechanics, and small details in how a book is categorized or described can affect whether the right reader ever sees it. This service involves a careful review of your book's publicly visible information: categories, keywords, description, and overall presentation on Amazon and Goodreads.

What you can expect: a written review of what's currently visible, where it may be limiting discovery, and practical suggestions for improvement. I do not promise ranking improvements, review counts, or placement outcomes, since those are controlled by the platforms themselves, not by any marketing service.

Request a Visibility Review

This Service Addresses

  • Categories or keywords that may not reflect the book accurately
  • Descriptions that don't clearly communicate the book's appeal
  • Uncertainty about how the book currently appears to browsing readers
04
Creative & Digital

Book Trailers & Author Websites

A reader's first impression often happens online, through a trailer, a landing page, or an author site, before they ever read a page of the book. This service covers the development of promotional concepts for book trailers and the creation of professional, clearly structured author websites that present your work the way it deserves to be presented.

What you can expect: concept development aligned with your book's tone and genre, a clear plan for any trailer or digital asset, and a finished digital experience that is easy for readers to navigate and understand.

Discuss Creative Concepts

This Service Addresses

  • No clear digital presence to direct readers toward
  • A website or trailer that doesn't reflect the book's quality
  • Difficulty translating the book's tone into visual or video form
05
Campaigns

Book Launch & Promotional Campaigns

Launch timing matters, and so does sequencing. This service covers the planning of coordinated promotional activity appropriate to your book's publication stage, whether that's a pre-launch period, a release week, or a relaunch of a backlist title. The plan reflects your genre, audience, and goals rather than a fixed template.

What you can expect: a staged promotional plan with clear timing, a list of recommended activities suited to your specific book and budget, and coordination support throughout the campaign window.

Plan Your Campaign

This Service Addresses

  • Uncertainty about what to do before, during, and after launch
  • Promotional activity that feels scattered or last-minute
  • A backlist title that could benefit from a relaunch push
06
Audience Development

Reader Outreach & Audience Development

Finding the right readers often means looking beyond paid advertising toward genuine communities where your genre's readers already gather. This service involves identifying relevant reader communities, book clubs, genre-specific spaces, and appropriate discovery opportunities for your book specifically.

What you can expect: a list of relevant, vetted opportunities for your book, and an outreach approach that is personalized, respectful of each community's norms, and compliant with the relevant platform's rules. I do not engage in mass messaging, spam tactics, or any outreach that disregards platform permissions.

Discuss Reader Outreach

This Service Addresses

  • Not knowing where genre-relevant readers actually gather
  • Concern about outreach that feels impersonal or inappropriate
  • A desire to build genuine reader relationships over time
